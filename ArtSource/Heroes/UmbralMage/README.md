# Ione Vaska / Iron Hierophant

Original geometry rebuilt around the supplied reference: faceless angular steel helmet with a crossbar and crimson pennants, broad sloping shoulders, narrow sealed torso, rib plates, pointed segmented leg armor and three pale crystal talons. The slim staff has a compact dark crescent head with a small violet crystal. No source photograph or stock asset is included.

Build with `python Tools/UmbralMage/build_umbral_mage.py` (NumPy, Pillow). Separate anatomy and staff groups retain the existing renderer identities and 18-bone animation rig. The rigid split front armor follows separate thigh and shin bones; there is no robe or cape. Existing staff contact markers preserve the grip and spell direction.

Validated finite mesh coordinates/normals, triangle indices, bone indices, separate body/staff groups and geometry budgets. Front and back images are offline renders of the exported mesh, not Unity screenshots. Unity compilation, animated intersections, actual materials and device performance still require an editor/device check.
