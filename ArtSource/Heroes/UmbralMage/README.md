# Ione Vaska / Pearl Mech

Rebuilt from the new user reference: pearl ceramic armor, a violet mechanical frame, compact multi-sensor helmet and antenna, exposed dark shoulder joints and pearl upper-arm plates, luminous violet hip reactors and angular mechanical legs. Heavy proportions widen the chest by 20%, thicken the abdominal core by 38%, and increase arm/leg cross-sections by 25%/23% around unchanged joint axes. The former robe, ritual crossbar and staff are removed. A four-petal deployed arcane focus replaces the staff; its geometry is fixed in the deployed pose, not animated deployment.

Build with `python Tools/UmbralMage/build_umbral_mage.py` (NumPy, Pillow). Body and weapon retain separate mesh groups, existing renderer identities and 18 rig bones. Legacy Staff marker names remain for compatibility but now mark the focus grip, aperture and orientation.

Generator validates finite vertices/normals, triangle and bone indices, geometry budgets and separate body/weapon triangles. Front/back images are offline geometry previews. Unity compilation, live animation clipping, actual materials and performance remain unverified.

The pink luminous shoulder caps are removed. Front and rear upper-arm plates lie between shoulder and elbow, following each upper-arm bone. Each front plate carries a narrow fluorescent white stripe with its own emissive material. Runtime mirrors the source focus onto the left hand, rotates its barrel forward and corrects reflected winding; source geometry previews still show the canonical weapon orientation.

The hand-held focus is 30% larger, scaled uniformly around its handle so the palm contact stays fixed; its aperture and orientation markers use the matching enlarged positions. The head replaces the plain antenna with a split pearl crown, violet channels, temple fins and a cyan forehead reactor while keeping the established white, violet and cyan palette.

Runtime wrist connections close the gap between the ceramic forearms and the offset palms. Two flexible cuffs add 160 vertices and 256 triangles to the body renderer using the existing sealed-black-core material. The terminal rings overlap the forearm shells and palm interiors; intermediate rings blend between the forearm and hand bones. Weights are sorted by strength and the body renderer uses two-bone skinning so mobile quality settings retain the blend. The connection follows wrist rotation and hand resizing without changing bone positions, weapon geometry or the source resource. These additions require no new renderer, material or per-frame mesh updates.

The complete production appearance class compiles and passes deterministic geometry/skinning fixtures using the generator's exact source mesh. Checks cover attachment rings inside both existing surfaces, unchanged original body triangle/material counts, no cuff geometry in the weapon renderer, normalized weights/normals, mesh offsets and nonuniform ancestor scales. Both endpoints remain attached across 96 combinations of elbow angle, wrist angle and hand scale (including the current 1.6x hands). This is not a Unity render or a live animation check; menu, movement and spell poses still require in-engine visual verification.
