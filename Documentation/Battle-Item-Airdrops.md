# Battle item team supplies

In offline play, the equipped local item is captured with the local player team when the match starts. In co-op/online play, every human player's equipped battle item is copied into the verified match plan. At 120 seconds on the DimensionsFront gameplay clock, each equipped human player can produce one supply crate, for up to five separate drops per team. Each player's own device consumes only that player's planned item; bots never create inventory drops. There is no ultimate activation hook. Pausing the match pauses both the clock and descent.

Offline landing candidates remain mirrored for teams 0/1 at x +/-8, +/-10 or +/-12. Network matches reserve five distinct team lanes at z -14, -7, 0, 7 and 14 with x fallbacks at +/-8, +/-10 and +/-12. Small per-lane z fallbacks keep a blocked crate inside its own reserved lane, so two team-mate supplies cannot select the same landing point. Bases are at x +/-36. If a slot has no clear landing candidate, that slot retries without affecting the other four.

An airborne crate cannot be collected. After landing it persists without a lifetime limit until a living hero of the owner team comes within 1.35m. Enemy heroes, dead heroes, minions and monsters cannot claim it. Any allied hero, including an allied bot, can claim the single crate. Effects affect the collector. Leaving/resetting/ending the match cleans up the supply. Inventory is consumed when the drop successfully starts, not on pickup; matches ending before two minutes do not consume it.

Regen restores 20% maximum health to the collector; Aegis grants a 20% maximum-health shield for five seconds; Velocity grants 10% movement speed for five seconds. Team Heal restores exactly 20 HP to every living allied hero when any allied hero collects the crate. Existing item-ID 4 inventory is preserved, so former Meteor items become Team Heal items after the update. The canopy, cords, landing marker, beveled crate and colored item symbols are built procedurally. Menu and collection descriptions and the match HUD use the new rules.

Private co-op and online matches now carry the equipped item in the verified roster. Authoritative gameplay applies pickup effects only on the host/dedicated server; clients create the same deterministic supply presentation and consume only their own device-local item. Device-local wallets remain client-side inventory, so competitive inventory validation would still require a server-owned item ledger.

## Validation

C# syntax, source rule checks, mirrored placement bounds and parachute triangle geometry are checked here. Unity is unavailable, so compilation, runtime mesh/material imports, timing, inventory persistence and touch/gameplay behavior require a Unity run.

Unity acceptance: equip each item; start a match; cast an ultimate before two minutes and verify no consumption/drop; pause around 1:59; resume and observe the planned descents at 2:00; verify no pickup in the air; leave crates for several minutes; try collecting with an enemy, then an allied bot/player; verify one effect and one inventory decrement per equipped human player. Test a full five-human team with five items and confirm five distinct landing lanes, plus a full 5v5 with ten total supplies, blocked landing candidates, Team Heal with multiple injured allies and exactly +20 HP per living ally without exceeding max HP, match end before 2:00, and reset/return-to-menu cleanup.
