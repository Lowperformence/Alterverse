# Visual upgrade review — 2 October 2026

## Implemented in dev/vertical-slice

- Ranger: reference-based body, two-hand rifle alignment, portrait framing correction.
- Tank: reference-based armor, shield and hammer; shield aligned forwards during attacks.
- Ione: new hooded armor, crystal staff and articulated light leg plates; long robe removed.
- Tahla: fitted leg plates and removal of both long front vestments and upper back mantle; additional arm details.
- Brakk: alternating forward punches; bronze helmet with red crest replaces the earlier wrestling mask.
- Traveler: new enclosed graphite/cyan exosuit and an entirely new orb mesh with three free rings. No old weapon vertices are retained. The orb floats above the hand, including in the shop.
- Remaining exposed hero limb areas: fitted upper-arm, forearm, hand, thigh and shin detail plates, using the existing bone influences. Existing fully armored replacement models retain their authored panels.
- Mechanical scorpion, magenta GJ golem, PSR crystal titan, skeletal Mars monster, Green Stalker alien and Noxara clockwork spider rebuilt. Skeleton and alien use surface placement. GJ/PSR/skeleton upper arms and forearms are exactly 70% of their previous lengths.
- Scorpion, Neptune and PSR presentation are initialized synchronously during spawn, removing the known manager-delay flash.
- Standard weapons remain visible beneath a translucent fluorescent color layer.
- Map edges: recessed fractures, faceted banks and hanging cliff fragments. Planet materials use distinct detail scales and additional fine grain.
- Mechguards: mounted instrument ribs, chest vents and rear cooling fins.
- Armor audio: Kaelon metallic latch/pot texture layers and Elyra creak layers removed; other classes, impacts, kill cue and music are not regenerated by this patch.
- Hotzone opening bursts removed for host and clients. Resolution itself contains no explosion call; reward spawning and score resolution retained.
- Bomber: flash, fireball, smoke, dust and ember layers replace the thin impact-only effect. Damage radius and timing unchanged.
- Difficulty: Expert receives normal damage; Nightmare is the harder tier with 4% incoming-damage reduction.

## External help researched

- **Cascadeur**: useful for refining keyframed weight, balance and follow-through. AutoPhysics suggests a physics-based variation of authored motion; FBX export is supported. This is the most relevant external editing aid for hero punches and casts. It has not been installed or used to author these commits.
  - https://cascadeur.com/help/tools/physics_tools/autophysics
  - https://cascadeur.com/help/getting_started/export_fbxdae
- **Blender Rigify**: modular limb rigs, including paw chains, suit creature-specific editing. Spider/scorpion rigs still require their own anatomy and contact constraints.
  - https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/addons/rigify/rig_types/limbs.html
- **Mixamo**: humanoid-only automatic rigging/animation according to Adobe; not a direct solution for eight-legged monsters. Existing custom movement was preserved rather than replaced with generic clips.
  - https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html
- **Poly Haven / ambientCG**: CC0 surface sources suitable for commercial game use and distribution. Good starting points for rock, gravel, sand and ice material studies. No external texture downloads were silently inserted into the project.
  - https://polyhaven.com/license
  - https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_ground
  - https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain
  - https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

Suggested art mapping: basalt and dark fractures for Pyraxis; dusty gravel for Mars/Moon; cracked ice for Kryon; crystal facets for PSR; coarse stone recolored magenta for GJ; moist organic ground for Noxara/Green Planet. These are art-direction choices, not claims about real exoplanet geology.
