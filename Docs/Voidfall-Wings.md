# VOIDFALL — fractured wing presentation

VOIDFALL now adds paired fractured violet wings to the equipped player and the actual 3D menu preview. Three tapered shards form each wing; obsidian-violet armor and restrained bright fracture edges preserve the existing hero body and team energy colors. Movement flexes the wings, idle motion stays subtle, and attacks briefly pulse them. Combat values, collision, navigation and hero roles are untouched.

Budget per equipped presentation: two mesh renderers, six short edge lines, one shared 24-vertex blade-cluster mesh, two owned materials; no particles, lights or colliders. Runtime-owned mesh/material resources are destroyed with the presentation. Bots do not acquire paid cosmetics automatically.

Gameplay-camera silhouette, occlusion, clipping on all six heroes, menu framing, paused motion and device draw-call cost: **NOT VERIFIED** in Unity. The implementation is an in-game asset change, not a concept image or a claim that final art approval has happened.
