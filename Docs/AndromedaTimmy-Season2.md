# Andromeda Timmy — Season 2 character

Small orange tabby cat with cinnamon stripes and baked fur fibers, in a detailed woven astronaut suit with visible ears/tail, cyan suit claws, twin-nozzle jet backpack and a round light-gray glass visor over his face. Assassin: short melee reach, Zhaev's running speed, lower health, one-target interruptible ultimate. Existing eight hero IDs and their combat numbers are preserved; Timmy is appended as ID 8.

## Availability and trying the hero

Open the project in Unity and let assets import. Select Andromeda Timmy directly in the existing Heroes menu and start a match. He is included in the regular nine-hero roster in both the editor and player builds; no preview switch is required. Run **DIMENSIONS FRONT → Checks → Andromeda Timmy integration (non-destructive)** to validate resources/catalogs in Unity.

Timmy is currently selectable through the existing default free-hero ownership path. A future purchase restriction is intentionally left to the owner; no shard price, purchase SKU or currency debit is introduced. Timmy has no purchasable cosmetics yet. Rebuild clients and the authoritative server from the same version. `HeroIdentity.HeroCount` remains a compile-time constant, as required by the existing audio bank.

The shorter capsule is offset downward to preserve the existing hero-origin/ground convention.

The requested earlier 100-point conversion could not be found in the repository or retrieved history. ## Scratch contact, grip and counterplay

Selection is synchronous on hold/tap/release; only enemy heroes qualify. Soldiers/objectives cannot hide a hero or receive the ultimate. The normal melee cone default is read from the existing `AimingSettings` (currently 70 degrees), including per-attack overrides. The shared preview displays that cone without adding a trajectory line; basic targeting remains unchanged.

Scratch captures the enemy's current position once. After a 0.2-second warning, Timmy traverses toward that fixed endpoint over 0.4 seconds. A 0.75 m stand-off keeps the actors from occupying the same spot. The leap does not home. On landing, range, living enemy eligibility, damage gate, authority and sight are checked again. A dodged/blocked leap gives no grip or damage and keeps its committed cooldown. An invalid initial cast consumes no cooldown.

Successful contact roots the victim's movement for two seconds and locks Timmy's movement/actions during the channel. **The victim can still attack Timmy**, allowing a counterattack or stun. Damage is four 13.5 ticks at 0.5-second intervals, not an immediate 54-damage soft-target hit. Each tick rechecks contact and authority. Caster stun, death, disable, authority loss, forced displacement, victim death/disable, damage immunity, obstruction or separation stops remaining ticks and releases both roots. Roots are source-owned; cleanup does not remove other slows/stuns/boosts or another source's grip. Existing short-stun immunity is unchanged.

NGO command aiming intent is reused. The host rejects owner-position updates during a root/leap. Movement-only victim roots are represented by zero movement with action permission; the channel uses the existing action-lock/stun snapshot field. Timmy's owner receives the host's channel position. Existing timestamped combat-motion events relay scratch ticks and contact VFX/audio, including to the casting client, without client-side damage simulation. Existing packet layouts remain intact. A separate `DF_COOP_TIMMY_FX_V1` presentation cue (52 bytes: round fingerprint, slot/kind, two positions, radius, duration, server time) relays warnings, fog, grenade flight, jet exhaust, leap claws and cancellation to clients. It validates sender, round, actor, finite coordinates and lifetimes, backdates late effects and discards expired cues. Clients do not rebroadcast. This transport still requires a real two-peer latency test.

## Art, animation and effects

- Editable model: `ArtSource/Heroes/AndromedaTimmy/AndromedaTimmy.blend`.
- Unity model: `Assets/Alterverse/Resources/AuthoredHeroes/Timmy.fbx`; 15,548 triangles, one anatomy, two claw renderers and a separate glass visor. The existing 19 hero bones remain, with one additional Tail bone under Hips; no extra renderer or alternate retarget pipeline. Claws are fully weighted to their matching WeaponGrip bone; palm/contact sockets coincide.
- Visor: a smooth circular spherical cap fitted to the existing helmet rim, with enough depth to cover the muzzle and whiskers. `DF_Visor` is fully weighted to Head, so it follows the existing head animation. Light-gray RGB is (0.78, 0.80, 0.82), opacity 0.20 = **80% transparency**. Blender source and FBX contain the same glass material; the existing hero importer configures transparent URP Lit alpha blending, front faces, no depth writing and no opaque shadow casting. The single surface avoids applying the gray tint twice. Importer version 15 refreshes cached hero imports. The helmet also has a deeper dome, white chin guard, graphite vent and side hinges inspired by the supplied astronaut reference. Native Unity/device rendering still requires visual confirmation.
- Five illustrated icons with opaque cosmic backgrounds replace the modeled prototypes in the existing runtime paths, retaining all GUIDs: 0 Claw Swipe, 1 Stardust, 2 Jet Pounce, 3 Scratch, 4 Claw. Sources are 1254px PNGs; Unity retains the existing 256px HUD import cap. Reusable artwork directions are in `Tools/AndromedaTimmy/icon-prompts.json`. `AndromedaTimmy-icons.blend` is only the earlier prototype source; it is not the source of these final illustrations.
- Fur and suit: orange/cinnamon tabby markings, cream muzzle, fine baked fibers and woven cloth. The suit has harness seams/buckles, chest controls, oxygen hoses, backpack hatch/indicator, pressure folds, thigh pockets, muted red leg bands and shin seams. `TimmyTextures/TimmySurface_Albedo.png` and `TimmySurface_Normal.png` are shared 1024px UV atlases with mipmaps and non-readable mobile imports; albedo is sRGB, normal is linear/normal-map import. The editable Blender source packs both textures. No hair particles are used.
- Tail: centered behind the pelvis in the rest pose, fully weighted to the dedicated Tail bone. The existing authored presentation applies a smooth side-to-side yaw after the final pose, using the model-up axis in the parent bone frame. It starts centered, sways ±30° over eight seconds and does not drive the pelvis or combat pose. `TailSwayDegrees` and `TailSwaySeconds` are central Timmy parameters.
- Stardust: a closed metal canister with a cyan energy rim flies along the existing pooled lob motion to the locked selected point during the original 0.3 s warning. The warning, damage circle and mist use the same 2 m radius. At landing the canister/warning clear and two translucent mist layers persist for 2 s, retaining opacity until the final 0.2 s fade. Damage/slow resolve once on landing; the lingering mist is cosmetic and adds no damage ticks.
- Jet Pounce: two cyan exhaust trails with amber cores attach to the actual `DF_TimmyJetL/R` Chest sockets for the 0.16 s dash and 0.12 s afterglow. The pool follows both nozzle position and rotation. Scratch also launches exactly three narrow flying claws ahead of Timmy, sharing the fixed 0.4 s leap path without homing or extra damage. Interruption clears airborne effects while preserving the final contact sweep.
- Studio reference: `AndromedaTimmy-preview.png`. This is a Blender render, not a screenshot of Unity gameplay.
- Common animation/retarget pipeline is reused. Basic claws alternate the existing slash clip. Scratch uses four contact-synchronous channel pulses; its action lock cannot suppress its own pose. Stun/death still interrupt it.
- Common pooled VFX are reused: cyan triple claw sweeps, sustained stardust mist, a visible grenade throw, socket-attached amber/cyan jet exhaust, target warning and flying/contact scratch claws. Cached Mist/Claw/TimmyGrenade meshes and Sustained/AttachedJet motions append to the existing enums without moving earlier indices. Rebuild assets from the repository root with Blender 4.5 LTS:

```bash
blender --background --python-exit-code 1 --python Tools/AndromedaTimmy/build_timmy.py
blender --background --python-exit-code 1 --python Tools/AndromedaTimmy/validate_timmy.py
```

The default build and `-- --model-only` regenerate the model, FBX, surface maps and studio preview while preserving the illustrated runtime icons. Optional `-- --prototype-icons` renders the old modeled prototypes into `ArtSource/Heroes/AndromedaTimmy/PrototypeIcons`, never into the runtime icon folder.

The generator uses original geometry/materials and CPU rendering. It writes model, FBX, packed surface textures and studio preview. The validator checks both Blender source and reimported FBX: bone names, normalized weights, rigid claws, head-bound visor, 80% glass transparency, circular silhouette, outward normals, actual face clearance, nondegenerate finite geometry, contact sockets, physical scale, triangle budget, centered tail weights, Chest jet sockets, textured fur/fabric and icon dimensions. Reports are committed alongside the art source.

## Verification and release checks

The production combat/input code is compiled against deterministic engine fixtures by `Tools/Aiming/Regression/run.py`, including the existing editor regressions. Timmy tests cover regular nine-hero availability, production audio compilation/dispatch, selected Q/W/R resource loading, one Stardust recording after its warning across multiple enemies, one Scratch recording across all four ticks and unchanged legacy cue indices, point sum, Zhaev speed reference, short basic range, single-target Q, multi-target/dodgeable W, reverse E, first-frame hero selection despite a closer soldier, tap/cancel, fixed-end dodge, two-second grip/four ticks, counterattack permission, caster/victim death, stun, disable, forced movement, wall rejection, snapshot semantics and source-owned root cleanup. Gameplay-clock first-damage samples: basic ~167 ms, Q ~175–200 ms, W 300 ms, Scratch ~1.1 s from cast (warning + leap + first tick). Zero dash timing in the fixture measures acceptance, not native traversal.

Final deterministic combat/input/audio/VFX run: **2,530 assertions passed**. The real `HeroVfxPool` scheduler/mesh builder and `HeroSkillVfx.Timmy` partial are compiled with engine fixtures: grenade geometry/flight/impact timing, exact mist radius, two-second visibility/final fade/no extra damage, moving/rotating nozzle attachment, afterglow, three fixed-flight claws, cancellation preserving contact sweeps and late-cue catch-up/expiry are checked. The common VFX facade, GPU and network transport remain fixtures in that harness. The upper-body/attack-pose regression also passes for the existing eight heroes (**294,488 assertions**). Blender source/FBX round-trip checks and five final illustrated icon resource/dimension checks were completed. These checks **do not constitute a Unity editor compile, native physics/rendering test, real multiplayer session or Android device test**.
