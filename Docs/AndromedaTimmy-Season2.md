# Andromeda Timmy — Season 2 character

Small orange tabby cat with cinnamon stripes and baked fur fibers, in a detailed woven astronaut suit with visible ears/tail, cyan suit claws, twin-nozzle jet backpack and a round light-gray glass visor over his face. Assassin: short melee reach, Zhaev's running speed, lower health, one-target interruptible ultimate. Existing eight hero IDs and their combat numbers are preserved; Timmy is appended as ID 8.

## Availability and trying the hero

Open the project in Unity and let assets import. Select Andromeda Timmy directly in the existing Heroes menu and start a match. He is included in the regular nine-hero roster in both the editor and player builds; no preview switch is required. Run **DIMENSIONS FRONT → Checks → Andromeda Timmy integration (non-destructive)** to validate resources/catalogs in Unity.

Timmy is currently selectable through the existing default free-hero ownership path. A future purchase restriction is intentionally left to the owner; no shard price, purchase SKU or currency debit is introduced. Timmy has no purchasable cosmetics yet. Rebuild clients and the authoritative server from the same version. `HeroIdentity.HeroCount` remains a compile-time constant, as required by the existing audio bank.

Light Paws passive: a successful damaging skill hit grants 12% speed for 1.2 seconds, at most once per 4 seconds. Basic hits and missed/rejected skill hits do not trigger it. AOE damage to several enemies triggers it once.

Raw base health is 185; the existing global multiplier produces **223.3875 HP** before his separate **50 HP rechargeable base shield**, which restores fully after 15 seconds without incoming damage. Speed is **6.374025 m/s**, exactly the current Zhaev reference. Basic DPS is approximately 28.21 versus Zhaev's 28.24; Timmy trades reach/durability for targeted mobility/control. Runtime model scale is 70%, collision radius 0.28 m and capsule height 1.4 m. The shorter capsule is offset downward to preserve the existing hero-origin/ground convention.

The requested earlier 100-point conversion could not be found in the repository or retrieved history. The user approved a **provisional design allocation using Zhaev as reference**: damage 30, mobility 28, control 24, durability 10, utility 8, total 100. This is a design budget, not a recovered stat conversion formula or proof of measured competitive balance. Matchup/win-rate tuning remains necessary.

## Scratch contact, grip and counterplay

Selection is synchronous on hold/tap/release; only enemy heroes qualify. Soldiers/objectives cannot hide a hero or receive the ultimate. The normal melee cone default is read from the existing `AimingSettings` (currently 70 degrees), including per-attack overrides. The shared preview displays that cone without adding a trajectory line; Timmy's automatic basic retains its selected target through the preparation and refreshes its position once at contact; melee attacks do not use projectile leading. Range, cone and wall checks still apply.

Scratch keeps the selected enemy identity through a 0.2-second warning, then samples an eligible enemy's position once at takeoff. The 0.4-second leap thereafter follows a fixed endpoint with a 0.75 m stand-off and cannot home. Contact with the selected enemy is checked during traversal, so approaching enemies are caught instead of jumped past; a point-blank cast can connect without requiring a nonzero dash. If no enemy is in the release cone/range, or the selected enemy leaves acquisition range before takeoff, Timmy still commits his directional leap and cooldown but that leap cannot damage or root anyone. Actual range, living enemy eligibility, damage gate, authority and line of sight are required for a targeted contact. Walls still block movement and targeted acquisition.

Successful contact roots **only the victim's movement for two seconds**. Timmy remains free to move, basic attack, Claw Swipe, Stardust and Jet Pounce; the victim can counterattack. Damage is four 13.5 ticks at 0.5-second intervals. Once contact succeeds, ordinary caster movement, separation and new casts do not shorten the hold or discard its remaining ticks. Caster stun, death, disable, authority loss, forced displacement, victim death/disable or damage immunity still stop remaining ticks and release the source-owned victim root. Cleanup leaves unrelated slows, stuns, boosts and roots intact. Scratch does not cancel an already queued basic attack.

NGO aiming intent is reused. The host rejects owner-position updates during the leap or a genuine root, while an unrooted Timmy's ordinary movement remains accepted after contact. The victim's root uses the existing zero movement multiplier with actions allowed. No caster action-lock snapshot is created. Existing timestamped combat-motion events relay scratch ticks and contact VFX/audio without client-side damage simulation. The existing 52-byte `DF_COOP_TIMMY_FX_V1` presentation cue relays warnings, fog, grenade flight, jet exhaust, leap claws and cancellation. Kind 6 ends only Scratch's airborne effects so a concurrent Stardust grenade keeps flying; kind 4 retains the full interruption cleanup. Sender, actor, round, finite coordinates and original cue lifetimes are validated. Both peers must use this revision; native two-peer transport still requires verification.

## Art, animation and effects

The existing 19 hero bones remain, with one additional Tail bone under Hips; no extra renderer or alternate retarget pipeline. Claws are fully weighted to their matching WeaponGrip bone; palm/contact sockets coincide.
- Feline proportions follow the supplied paw and cat-face references. Each forepaw is a single closed, flattened oval surface with a tapered wrist and four scalloped toe tips; its 18.5 cm length exceeds its 13.06 cm width. Both paws remain fully Hand-weighted and the existing three cyan suit claws begin inside the distal paw surface. The head has fuller cheek contours, a tapered lower jaw, a projecting paired cream muzzle, a small warm triangular nose, a feline mouth, wider-spaced almond eyes with slit pupils, longer fanned whiskers and broader triangular ears. Facial geometry remains Head-weighted and the face fits behind the existing transparent visor.
- Visor: a smooth circular spherical cap fitted to the existing helmet rim, with enough depth to cover the muzzle and whiskers. `DF_Visor` is fully weighted to Head, so it follows the existing head animation. Light-gray RGB is (0.78, 0.80, 0.82), opacity 0.20 = **80% transparency**. Blender source and FBX contain the same glass material; the existing hero importer configures transparent URP Lit alpha blending, front faces, no depth writing and no opaque shadow casting. Rebuild assets from the repository root with Blender 4.5 LTS:

```bash
blender --background --python-exit-code 1 --python Tools/AndromedaTimmy/build_timmy.py
blender --background --python-exit-code 1 --python Tools/AndromedaTimmy/validate_timmy.py
```

The default build and `-- --model-only` regenerate the model, FBX, surface maps and studio preview while preserving the illustrated runtime icons. Optional `-- --prototype-icons` renders the old modeled prototypes into `ArtSource/Heroes/AndromedaTimmy/PrototypeIcons`, never into the runtime icon folder.

The generator uses original geometry/materials and CPU rendering. It writes model, FBX, packed surface textures and studio preview. The validator checks both Blender source and reimported FBX: bone names, normalized weights, rigid claws, head-bound visor, 80% glass transparency, circular silhouette, outward normals, actual face clearance, nondegenerate finite geometry, contact sockets, physical scale, triangle budget, centered tail weights, Chest jet sockets, textured fur/fabric and icon dimensions. It also measures symmetric paw proportions, four toe tips, closed connected paw topology, claw contact, almond-eye aspect ratio, nose placement and muzzle projection. Reports are committed alongside the art source.

## Verification and release checks

The production combat/input code is compiled against deterministic engine fixtures by `Tools/Aiming/Regression/run.py`, including the existing editor regressions. Zero dash timing in the fixture measures acceptance, not native traversal.

Final deterministic combat/input/audio/VFX run: **2,601 assertions passed**. The real `HeroVfxPool` scheduler/mesh builder and `HeroSkillVfx.Timmy` partial are compiled with engine fixtures: grenade geometry/flight/impact timing, exact mist radius, two-second visibility/final fade/no extra damage, moving/rotating nozzle attachment, afterglow, three fixed-flight claws, cancellation preserving contact sweeps and late-cue catch-up/expiry are checked. The upper-body/attack-pose regression also passes for the existing eight heroes (**294,488 assertions**).
